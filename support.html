How can we help?

For account, billing, installation, or optimization issues, send the steps you followed and the exact message shown by PromptIQ.

The floating PromptIQ button is missing

Refresh the AI platform tab after installing or updating PromptIQ. Confirm the current page is ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, or DeepSeek.

Sign in or sign up is not completing

Check your connection, use a valid email address, and use a password between 8 and 128 characters. Open the PromptIQ toolbar popup again and retry. Existing accounts should use Sign in.

The extension still looks like an older version

Chrome Web Store updates roll out only after the submitted version is approved. Open Chrome's Extensions page, enable Developer mode, and choose Update to request the newest approved release.

Premium checkout is unavailable

Premium billing is being connected to Razorpay. Free Smart Template optimization remains available while billing setup is completed.

How do daily limits work?

Free includes 100 local Smart Template optimizations per day and 5 cloud AI trials per day after sign-in. Premium includes 50 cloud AI optimizations and 200 local Smart Template optimizations per day.
