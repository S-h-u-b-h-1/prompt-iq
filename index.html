A few good questions

Before you
start writing.

Do I need an account?

No. Local scoring and Smart Template work without an account. Sign in to use your five daily cloud AI trials and synchronize your history.

Where does my prompt go?

Local Smart Template keeps your draft on your device. Choosing cloud AI sends your prompt to PromptIQ's server and its AI provider for optimization. Signed-in history is synchronized. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Will it work with my AI subscription?

PromptIQ works beside the chat input in supported platforms. It does not replace or include a ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or other AI service subscription.

Can I change the optimized prompt?

Yes. You can edit it, compare it with your original, or undo the replacement. Your prompt is still yours.
