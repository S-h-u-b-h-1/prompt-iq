Privacy Policy for PromptIQ

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PromptIQ collects, uses, and shares your data when you use the PromptIQ Chrome Extension and associated services.

1. What Data We Collect

To provide prompt optimization and scoring, PromptIQ collects the following data types:

  • Prompt Text (Personal Communications): PromptIQ reads supported AI chat composers after you sign in and accept the in-extension disclosure. Quick Rewrite runs on your device. When you run a rewrite, the original prompt, result, and scores are saved to your account. When available, AI Rewrite sends the selected prompt to the recipients shown beside its send button.
  • Optional Draft Sync: If enabled, PromptIQ saves the latest revision of each draft after a typing pause, including unsent text. You can score and rewrite prompts with draft sync off in version 1.0.17. Earlier versions that offered draft sync required separate draft consent to activate the optimizer.
  • Website Content: PromptIQ reads prompt text from supported chat composers to calculate your score. It does not collect unrelated form fields, chat responses, page titles, or conversation URLs.
  • Optional Saved Searches: If enabled in Dashboard > History, completed searches in PromptIQ's prompt library are saved with their category, result count, and time. This does not collect browser search history or searches made on other websites.
  • User Activity Metadata: We collect metadata about optimization events (such as the AI platform name, the original score, the optimized score, and the time of the event) to populate your history logs and dashboard statistics.
  • Account Information: An account is required to use the extension. We collect your email address and password authentication data. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Product Analytics: We record event names for account actions, rewrite success or failure, prompt insertion, favorites, and other product actions to measure reliability and usage. Signed-in events may be associated with your account ID. The events do not include prompt text, email addresses, AI responses, or the URLs of AI sites you visit.

2. How Data Is Stored

  • Local Storage: PromptIQ keeps your settings, sign-in session, on-device allowance, and favorites in Chrome extension storage. Favorites are stored on this Chrome profile.
  • Database Storage: Original and optimized prompts, scores, the latest revisions of active prompt drafts, and consent settings are synchronized to our Neon Postgres database through our HTTPS backend under your account. PromptIQ library searches are stored only when you separately enable that optional setting. The website's local demo remains on your device.

3. How Data Is Used

We use the collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • To calculate your prompt scores in real-time.
  • To perform user-requested AI Rewrite optimizations.
  • To save and synchronize your optimization runs history so you can retrieve them in your extension popup.
  • To save recent prompt drafts for account history and recovery.
  • To operate the service, prevent abuse, understand aggregate feature use, and process user-requested feedback.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.
  • Service providers: Vercel hosts the PromptIQ server and Neon stores account data. Quick Rewrite does not contact an AI provider. When AI Rewrite is available, the extension names the model provider before you choose to send the prompt through our FreeLLMAPI gateway. The request contains the prompt, selected platform, and optimization instructions. It excludes your password, account email, and conversation URL. PromptIQ uses provider configurations that permit processing only to deliver the requested rewrite.
  • Voluntary Contributions: If you choose the optional UPI contribution link, your UPI app and payment providers process the payment under their own terms. PromptIQ does not receive your bank login credentials or full bank account details.
  • Website Analytics: When configured, Google Analytics receives page and product event names from the public website. Prompt text is not sent to Google Analytics, and advertising personalization signals are disabled in PromptIQ's configuration.
  • Chrome Web Store Analytics: Google may provide aggregate Store listing traffic and installation analytics to PromptIQ through the Chrome Web Store developer tools.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Optional drafts and library searches are available for 30 days from their last saved timestamp. Expired entries are excluded from retrieval and removed on the next saved-activity read or write for your account. Use "Delete drafts & searches" in Dashboard > History to remove these account records and turn both settings off. Infrastructure backups may persist within the provider's backup retention window.

Use "Clear All" in the History tab of the PromptIQ dashboard to delete optimization history from your account. Remove local favorites in the Favorites tab. Account and operational logs may be retained as needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. You can request account deletion using the contact address below.

6. Policy Certifications

  • We do not sell user data or transfer it outside the service providers needed for PromptIQ's user-requested optimization, storage, hosting, authentication, and analytics functions.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to PromptIQ's single core function.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not use prompt, account, or browsing data for personalized advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read prompt data except with the user's explicit consent for support, when necessary for security, or when required by law.
  • The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
