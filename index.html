A few good questions

Before you
start writing.

Do I need an account?

The website demo and Paste & rewrite in version 1.0.18 do not require an account. In-chat scoring, insertion, and synced history use a free account.

Where does my prompt go?

The paste editor does not send or save your text. When you rewrite inside a supported chat, the original and result are saved to your account. You can choose to save unsent drafts for up to 30 days. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Will it work with my AI chat?

Use the floating badge on supported sites. On Copilot or another chat without the badge, open Paste & rewrite from the extension toolbar, then copy the result back. PromptIQ does not include an AI service subscription.

Can I change the optimized prompt?

Yes. You can edit it, compare it with your original, or undo the replacement. Your prompt is still yours.
